修复 App Store 版微信打补丁后打不开 / App Store edition launches again after patching

- 修复 App Store 版微信（如 build 269602）开启防撤回后「无法打开」：App Store 版带有一项只有 Apple 签名才能使用的受限权限（`com.apple.developer.team-identifier`），重新签名后系统拒绝启动。现在重签时会去掉这类受限权限。已在 App Store 原版副本上实测：旧版打完无法启动，新版打完可以正常启动。
- 已经被旧版弄得打不开的 App Store 微信：从 App Store 重新安装（或删除后重新获取），再用 1.0.8 开启一次即可。
- 官网版不受影响；官网最新版 4.1.15 打补丁后同样实测可以启动。

Fixes the App Store edition of WeChat (e.g. build 269602) refusing to open after anti-recall was turned on: it carries a restricted entitlement (`com.apple.developer.team-identifier`) that only an Apple signature may hold, so macOS rejected the re-signed app. Re-signing now drops such entitlements. Verified on a copy of the App Store original: the previous version's result would not launch; this version's does. If an earlier version left your App Store WeChat unable to open, reinstall it from the App Store and turn protection on again with 1.0.8. The direct-download edition is unaffected; a patched 4.1.15 was also verified to launch.

macOS 15+ · Apple Silicon + Intel app · ad-hoc signed, without Apple Developer ID signing or notarization.
