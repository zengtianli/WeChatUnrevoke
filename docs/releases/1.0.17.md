第二个微信 / Second WeChat

- 新增「第二个微信」（[WeChatTweak #11](https://github.com/zengtianli/WeChatTweak/issues/11)）：一键做出一份微信副本（`~/Applications/WeChat2.app`），有自己的登录和聊天记录，可与原来的微信同时打开。不额外占磁盘，主微信不受影响，创建时可以开着；副本继承当前的防撤回状态。
- 微信升级后，界面会提示副本版本落后，点「更新副本」按新版重做；聊天记录保存在副本之外，不受影响。重做后副本的签名身份不变，已给的截屏、麦克风、摄像头授权继续有效。
- 「移除…」把副本移到废纸篓，聊天记录保留在 `~/Library/Containers/com.tencent.xinWeChat2`。命令行同步提供 `unrevoke second status|make|open|remove`。
- 限制：官网版微信须是已收录的版本——副本要能关掉内置更新器，否则下次自动更新后会打不开，此时界面直接说明、不创建。副本不带应用沙盒（带着沙盒的副本与主微信同时打开会闪退），会在 `~/Documents/app_data/` 下写日志与崩溃记录；首次用到截屏、麦克风、摄像头时各授权一次。
- 已在微信 4.1.15.22（官网版、Apple Silicon、SIP 关闭）实测副本与主微信同时运行；SIP 开启的机器尚待反馈。同时登录多个账号是否符合微信的使用规则，请自行判断。
- 本版没有新增补丁点，支持的微信版本不变。

New: Second WeChat ([WeChatTweak #11](https://github.com/zengtianli/WeChatTweak/issues/11)). One click makes a copy of WeChat at `~/Applications/WeChat2.app` with its own login and chat history, so two accounts can be open at once. It takes no extra disk space, the first WeChat is not changed and may stay open while the copy is made; the copy inherits the current anti-recall state. After a WeChat update the window says the copy is behind and Update the copy rebuilds it from the new version; chat history lives outside the copy and is kept, and the copy keeps its signing identity, so Screen Recording, Microphone and Camera grants stay valid. Remove… moves the copy to the Trash and keeps its chat history in `~/Library/Containers/com.tencent.xinWeChat2`. The command line offers the same as `unrevoke second status|make|open|remove`. Limits: a direct-download WeChat has to be a supported version, because a copy only lasts when its built-in updater can be blocked; otherwise the window says so and nothing is created. The copy runs without the App Sandbox (a sandboxed copy crashes when both are open) and writes logs and crash records under `~/Documents/app_data/`; it asks once for Screen Recording, Microphone and Camera on first use. Verified side by side with the first WeChat on 4.1.15.22 (direct download, Apple Silicon, SIP disabled); SIP-enabled Macs still need feedback. Whether signing in to several accounts is acceptable under WeChat's rules is your call. No patch locations or supported WeChat builds change in this release.

macOS 13+ · Apple Silicon + Intel · ad-hoc signed, without Apple Developer ID signing or notarization.
